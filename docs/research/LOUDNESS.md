- `keys`: C2 bass note, C and F/A chords, an eight-note melody (C5–A5), sustain pedal down 0–4 s.
- `lead`: monophonic eight-note melody C4–C5. `bass`: monophonic bass line G1–F2. `drums`: two bars of kick / snare / closed hat + crash at 120 BPM.

Each render is followed by a 2 s tail (inside the gated measurement). Presets ending in a master `limiter` may be driven into it; without one the gain stops at the true-peak limit (`peak-limited`, accepted by `--check`: only dynamics processing could make that crest factor louder). Anything else off target, e.g. needing more than the +12 dB `volume_db` limit, is `OUT` and fails `--check`.

## Notes

- "Before" is the factory state at the time of the first normalization (format-2 rewrite included). The Doors
  *People Are Strange* and Floyd *Echoes* presets used a master `gain` (+11.7 dB) before their limiter because
  `volume_db` used to be applied after the master chain; `volume_db` now goes before a trailing limiter
  (ARCHITECTURE §5.3), so *People Are Strange* carries the level in `volume_db` (before = 11.7). *Echoes* needs
  more than the +12 dB `volume_db` limit, so it keeps a +6 dB `gain` slot (before = 5.7 + 6).
- `drums/big-rusty-kit`: its master limiter ceiling went from −1 to −2 dB, since the limiter (no look-ahead) lets
  inter-sample drum transients through to about −0.9 dBTP; it still sits at the +12 dB `volume_db` limit.
- Only `bells-keys/ich-will-cold-pluck` is pushed noticeably into its own master limiter (about 2.4 dB more gain
  than loudness gained); everywhere else the gain is linear within 1 dB.
- `strings/vpo-string-ensemble`: the +5 dB lifted the samples' DC offset over the render suite's 1e-3 limit, so
  a master `eq` 20 Hz high-pass (inaudible, loudness unchanged) was added before its limiter.
- The `[samples]` drum-kit tail criterion is now relative (tail ≥ 20 dB below the render's RMS): normalized kits
  ring louder in absolute terms.
- `peak-limited` presets (fast plucks without a master limiter) are as loud as the −1 dBTP ceiling allows.
